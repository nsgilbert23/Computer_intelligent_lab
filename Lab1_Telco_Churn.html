Lab 1 – Telco Customer Churn: Data Preprocessing & Exploration¶

i. Introduction¶

Customer churn means a customer stops using a company's service. Keeping an existing customer is usually cheaper than winning a new one, so telecom companies want to know who leaves and why.

In this lab I use the Telco Customer Churn dataset (7,043 customers, 21 attributes) from Kaggle. The work has three parts:

  1. Load the dataset and inspect its first and last rows.
  2. Preprocess it: encode Churn as 1/0, check and fix data types, and find and remove null values.
  3. Explore how churn relates to demographics (gender, senior citizen, partner, dependents), services (phone service) and payments (contract, paperless billing, payment method), and interpret every output.

v. Data exploration¶

In [9]:
def churn_rate_plot(col, title=None, order=None):
    """Show counts and churn rate (%) for each category of `col`, with two charts."""
    ct = pd.crosstab(df[col], df["Churn"])
    ct.columns = ["Stayed", "Churned"]
    ct["Total"] = ct.sum(axis=1)
    ct["Churn rate (%)"] = (ct["Churned"] / ct["Total"] * 100).round(2)
    display(ct)
    fig, ax = plt.subplots(1, 2, figsize=(12, 4))
    sns.countplot(data=df, x=col, hue="Churn", order=order, ax=ax[0], palette=["#4C78A8", "#E45756"])
    ax[0].set_title(f"{title or col}: customers by churn")
    ax[0].legend(title="Churn", labels=["Stayed", "Churned"])
    rate = ct["Churn rate (%)"].reindex(order) if order else ct["Churn rate (%)"]
    bars = ax[1].bar(rate.index.astype(str), rate.values, color="#E45756")
    ax[1].bar_label(bars, fmt="%.1f%%")
    ax[1].axhline(df["Churn"].mean() * 100, ls="--", c="grey", label="Overall churn (26.6%)")
    ax[1].set_title(f"{title or col}: churn rate"); ax[1].set_ylabel("Churn rate (%)"); ax[1].legend()
    plt.setp(ax[0].get_xticklabels(), rotation=15); plt.setp(ax[1].get_xticklabels(), rotation=15)
    plt.tight_layout(); plt.show()

Summary of findings¶

Factor Churn pattern Strength
Gender ≈27.0% vs 26.2% None
Senior citizen 41.7% vs 23.7% Strong
Partner 33.0% (no) vs 19.7% (yes) Moderate
Dependents 31.3% (no) vs 15.5% (yes) Moderate–strong
Phone service 26.7% vs 25.0% Weak
Contract 42.7% / 11.3% / 2.8% Very strong
Paperless billing 33.6% vs 16.4% Strong

Conclusion: Churn (about 26.6% overall) is driven mainly by contract type and payment method, and is higher among seniors, single customers without dependents and paperless-billing customers. Gender and phone service matter little. These findings suggest focusing retention efforts on month-to-month, electronic-check customers, and encouraging longer contracts and automatic payments.
